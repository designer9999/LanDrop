# LanDrop 2.0.1 — chat over Tailscale from anywhere

Use LanDrop with a colleague on a different internet connection, as long as you
both are connected to the same Tailscale network.

- **Phones over Tailscale.** Android now accepts LanDrop connections that arrive
  through its Tailscale tunnel. A phone discovers a desktop as soon as the desktop
  reaches it, and remembers that device so they find each other again after
  restarts. Connections from mobile-carrier addresses outside the tunnel are still
  refused.
- **Faster reconnects.** A colleague who comes online now appears within about two
  minutes instead of up to fifteen. Rescan finds them immediately.
- **Windows Firewall helper.** Settings → Tailscale shows whether Tailscale is
  connected and offers **Allow** to let LanDrop receive messages on your LAN and
  Tailscale networks. Windows often treats Tailscale as a public network, which
  silently blocked incoming messages. The rule asks for Windows permission once and
  is limited to LanDrop, port 29171 and your local subnet plus the Tailscale range.
- **Clean upgrade badge.** History saved by 1.8.x is treated as read, so upgrading
  no longer shows every old message as unread.

LanDrop still never calls Tailscale's control interface on Windows; discovery uses
Windows network routes and LanDrop's own identity check, so it cannot affect your
Tailscale or Mullvad connection.
